About

The person
behind the work.

Growing up, Motunrayo Akinsete was surrounded by people who were sharp, hardworking, and full of potential. What they often lacked was access. To the right tools, the right rooms, the right information at the right time. She never stopped thinking about that.

It is why she pursued AI research — not as an abstract academic exercise, but because she wanted to understand the technology well enough to make it useful for the people who needed it most. She completed her MSc as a Google DeepMind Scholar at the University of Birmingham, where her research focused on responsible AI and fairness in large language models.

But sitting with research was never going to be enough for her. She wanted to be in the room. So she started consulting with businesses figuring out how to use AI without the overwhelm. She began training teams and professionals who needed someone who could explain the hard things simply without dumbing them down. She started speaking at events where the conversation about AI and Africa needed someone who actually lived both.

She founded AI Accelerator Academy because she saw too many talented people circling the same confusion with no clear way in.

What drives her has not changed since the beginning. She wants people to be better off because they crossed paths with her work.

Research

Published work
and active inquiry.

Mo is an active AI/ML researcher whose work focuses on responsible AI, fairness in language models, and the equitable development of AI systems for underserved communities.

Research Focus

What Mo studies.

⚖️

Responsible AI

The ethical development and deployment of AI systems, with emphasis on accountability, transparency, and harm prevention.

🔍

LLM Fairness

Bias measurement and mitigation in large language models. Ensuring AI systems produce equitable outputs across demographic groups.

🌍

AI for Global Health

How AI systems can serve marginalised and underserved communities, particularly in African and low-resource contexts.

Publications & Projects

Selected research output.

Paper · In Progress

DeployStega: Covert LLM Communication via GitHub

A novel system for covert communication between large language models using GitHub's collaborative environment as a steganographic channel. Targeting ACL conference format. Co-authored with research collaborators including Srikanth, Menon, and Howes.

Research Programme · Completed

LLM Fairness — Algoverse AI Research Program

Research into bias, fairness, and equity in large language models. Completed as part of the Algoverse AI Research Program. Focuses on measuring and mitigating demographic bias in model outputs.

Journal Publication · 2020
