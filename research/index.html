Mo is an active AI/ML researcher whose work focuses on responsible AI, fairness in language models, and the equitable development of AI systems for underserved communities.

Research Focus

What Mo studies.

⚖️

Responsible AI

The ethical development and deployment of AI systems, with emphasis on accountability, transparency, and harm prevention.

🔍

LLM Fairness

Bias measurement and mitigation in large language models. Ensuring AI systems produce equitable outputs across demographic groups.

🌍

AI for Global Health

How AI systems can serve marginalised and underserved communities, particularly in African and low-resource contexts.
