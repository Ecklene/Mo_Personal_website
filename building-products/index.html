AI Product Management

Good decisions
make better products.

My approach to turning an AI idea
into a product people can use.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the real need.

Who is this for? What are they trying to do? Start with the user’s problem, the context and the outcome that matters.

02 / SHAPE & BUILD

Make the idea tangible.

Explore where AI adds value, set priorities and prototype the experience. Use feedback to refine what belongs in the product.

03 / EVALUATE

Measure what matters.

Think beyond model performance. Consider usefulness, reliability, fairness and the experience of the people using the product.
